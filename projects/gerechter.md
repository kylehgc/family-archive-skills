# Gerechter family archive

**Site:** [gerechter-family.pages.dev](https://gerechter-family.pages.dev)

This is an archive and memorial for the family of Armin Gerechter (1899–1955), who was born in Klein Räschen in Brandenburg and died in Toronto. It covers his parents, his ten siblings and their families, most of whom were murdered in the Holocaust. It was built over about eight days in November 2026, starting from a Geni tree and growing into archive copies from the Brandenburg State Archive, the Arolsen Archives, the Bundesarchiv, address books and other records.

## What's in it

- **Every document read is transcribed in full**: the original language, an English translation, and the facts extracted with a confidence grade. That is over 350 documents.
- **A master timeline**, with a date, place, person, event, grade and source on every row. It sits alongside person and place pages.
- **Short films, one per person.** Still charcoal-style drawings on a black stage are cut together with real documents and period photographs, set to Jewish music of their place and time, in English, German and Hebrew. The rules for the films are strict:
  - perpetrators are never shown, only their documents and the aftermath;
  - each life starts before 1933, so the persecution breaks into a life already known;
  - each film ends on the family that went on.
- **Records requests to archives** in Germany, Poland, Scandinavia and elsewhere, each logged along with the reply.
- **Privacy rules enforced by the site's build and tests.** Living relatives are not shown, and private research stays out of the site.

## What it taught

The costliest failures came from losing track, not from the research itself. One records order was drafted, forgotten when the session was summarised, and drafted again. Findings stayed in research notes and never reached the films. Facts drifted between films that had been written from summaries. The fixes were:

- a working log and a STATUS page, updated as things happen;
- a rule that a corrected fact isn't done until every place that shows it agrees;
- full transcriptions of everything read.
